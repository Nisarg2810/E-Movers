Concept prototype

This is a working mock-up, not a live system — nothing here is saved beyond this browser tab. It's meant to show what a digital move-quality workflow could look like for E-Movers' FIDI and ISO 9001-certified process: a condition checklist and sign-off per job, rolling into a branch-level quality view, in place of paper sheets or spreadsheets.
